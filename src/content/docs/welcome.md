These are Hack4Impact-UMDs engineering docs. They're meant to provide resources to support engineers and tech leads. 

These docs are evolving and open for anyone to edit. If you want to make additions, fixes, or edits feel free to make a PR on Github.

:::note
**New content contributions must be human written**. Documentation should be a source of truth for both humans and LLMs. Using LLM output dilutes its value and indicates a lack of respect for the reader.
:::

When you write a new article, credit yourself with the `authors` frontmatter field. It's always a list of entries with a `name`, even when there's only one author. Add an optional `url` to link your name to your site:

```yaml
---
title: My Article
description: What it covers
authors:
  - name: Jane Doe
  - name: John Smith
    url: https://johnsmith.dev
---
```

Authors show up under the article title.

To get started see the sidebar on the left for current articles or use the search functionality.
